ABOUT YOUR BOOK

A guide that says what it means.

This book uses the item list and files from Rina’s Linggango installation. It translates verified mechanics into everyday language.

Detailed explanation means the entry has a written guide based on the listed sources. General item type explains a familiar type of item; unique mod effects may still need checking. Needs research means we have the item’s name, but its behavior has not yet been explained reliably.

The item export is dated September 2, 2026. It has not been compared with a fresh live-game export. Recipes can also differ on a multiplayer server.

Artwork belongs to the original mod creators. Missing pictures are shown with a letter symbol. The book does not run Minecraft or change your save.
